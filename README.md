# Kviskre

Private dictation for Mac and iPhone: hold a key, speak, release, and the text lands in whatever app you are using.
Built for Norwegian, with NB-Whisper from the National Library of Norway (Bokmål, Nynorsk and spoken dialects), and
open to about 99 other languages with OpenAI's multilingual Whisper. Everything runs on your device; nothing leaves it.

Website: [www.kviskre.no](https://www.kviskre.no/) (norsk) · [www.kviskre.no/en](https://www.kviskre.no/en) (English) ·
